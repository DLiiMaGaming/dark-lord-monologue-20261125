# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

Entry for the make.fun competition
[u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb](https://make.fun/c/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

`main.py` writes a single 100-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt` — a fantasy
villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the rhythm,
the pauses and the small deflections, never in a plain statement of weariness.
The speaker is a specific villain with a history (fourteen years since the
hero's sword stuck in his floor) and a scheme (a war he keeps rescheduling).

## The monologue

> The hero's sword is still stuck in my floor. Fourteen years now. I keep
> meaning to pull it out, tomorrow, maybe, when the rain stops. My generals
> want a war. My war room wants a map. My map wants a coastline that no longer
> exists, because I moved it. I moved it twice, actually. The second time was
> spite. The kettle has boiled dry again. I can hear it ticking. Outside, the
> banners flap for a battle I scheduled last spring and keep rescheduling,
> like a dentist appointment. Let them come. The door is open. The sword is
> still there.

## Verification

- Exactly **100 words**, **534 characters** (gate: 1–600 characters)
- Deterministic: three runs produce byte-identical output
  (`sha256 2cfd1829fd1c1b8501b26b7b0857128ea4383d254ccda97234c7d9cb69b59cab`)

## Run

python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
